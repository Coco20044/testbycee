A Short Trip Into My Mind

under the glow of the crescent moon,she wandered-not through the forest, but through her own mind
she was both the dreamer and the dream, and most times often referred to herself as DREAM GIRL

each step she took echoed through memories wrapped in silence and secrets
within her, trees grew tall with those secrets, her paths wrapped and twisted in hope and darkness whispered not fear... but POWER.

SHE WAS NEVER LOST. SHE WAS EXPLORING. because sometimes,to find yourself, YOU MUST WALK DEEP INTO THE FOREST OF YOUR OWN SOUL
